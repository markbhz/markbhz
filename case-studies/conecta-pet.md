# Conecta Pet

**Stage:** Functional MVP  
**Category:** Mobile Marketplace · Realtime · Payments  
**Source code:** Private

## Context

Pet-care services require trust, clear scheduling and visibility during a walk. Those steps are often divided between messaging, maps and payment applications.

## Product

Conecta Pet brings pet profiles, walker discovery, bookings, chat, GPS tracking, notifications and payment confirmation into one mobile flow.

### Delivered capabilities

- Pet-owner and walker profiles
- Bookings and real-time chat
- GPS tracking during authorized walks
- Push notifications
- PIX, bank slip and card checkout in a sandbox environment
- Administrative and privacy flows

## My role

Product architecture, React Native implementation, Supabase integration, real-time flows, payment orchestration and privacy readiness.

## Technical direction

React Native and Expo Router connected to Supabase Auth, PostgreSQL, Storage and Realtime. Payment creation and webhook processing run in authenticated Edge Functions.

## Privacy

Portfolio demonstrations use synthetic profiles. Precise locations, payment credentials and personal records are excluded from public assets.
